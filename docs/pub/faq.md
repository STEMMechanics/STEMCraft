# FAQ

## Is STEMCraft invite-only?

No. The former invitation-code process and Coins economy are obsolete and have been removed from this handbook.

## Is there a cost to play?

The server is free to play. Separate STEMMechanics workshops may have their own booking cost.

## Can Java and Bedrock players join?

Yes. See [Connecting](/broken/pages/Y5WUZAwdljMfwT1pIrf2) and [Java & Bedrock](/broken/pages/f89oRaD6iJg4LzDHK6W8).

## Why did the server ask me a maths question?

The current plugin can hold a first-time player in a restricted session until they answer a generated addition or subtraction question in chat. This is an anti-bot check and not an invite code.

## Why does a custom item look ordinary?

Accept or resend the [Resource Pack](/broken/pages/gEBfbQIuBp8F6L9SpNSg).

## Are Waystones, Traders, and Survival Cities available?

They are planned, but their implementation is not present in this branch. Their pages are marked **Coming soon** until live behavior can be verified.

## Where are news, galleries, and staff details?

Those belong on STEMMechanics channels, social media, and Discord; the staff team belongs on the STEMMechanics STEMCraft page.

## Where can I get help?

See [Getting Help](safety-and-community/getting-help.md).

# Minecart Racing

Ride through every checkpoint and finish all laps. Right-click the feather for a boost every five seconds.

Dismounting or leaving the course returns you and a replacement cart to your last checkpoint. Pass every gate in order; the last gate counts a lap.

Use `/minecartrace list` to find an arena, `/minecartrace join <arena>` to play,
`/minecartrace spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/minecartrace leave` to return.

The HUD shows the countdown, your progress or score, and the round winner. At the
end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and you return
to where you joined from.

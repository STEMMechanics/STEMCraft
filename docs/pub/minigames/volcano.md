# Volcano

Survive as long as you can. Dodge erupting vents, lava flows and crater holes.
The last survivor wins; anyone still alive at the time limit shares the win.

Smoke and small rising flames warn you before a vent erupts. Eruptions vary
between scattered spurts, clustered bursts and lines of fissures. Lava shoots
upward as a damaging geyser, then spreads across the platform. Hot ground cools
into basalt or blackstone, while some eruptions leave holes.

Flame warnings, geysers, magma and lava damage your health on a controlled timer.
Lava and geysers keep you burning briefly after you leave them, and natural
healing is disabled during the round. Running out of health or falling below the
floor eliminates you and makes you a spectator. Keep moving onto safe ground
instead of waiting in a vent.

Wait in the lobby during the countdown; you move onto the floor when it ends.
By default, you then have 15 seconds to get ready before eruptions begin. The HUD
shows when play will start, and your survival timer begins at that point.

Use `/volcano list` to find an arena, `/volcano join <arena>` to play,
`/volcano spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/volcano leave` to return.

The HUD shows your survival time, remaining survivors and time left. Your timer
stops when you are eliminated, and you receive your survival time at the end.
Solo practice continues until you fall, lose your health or reach the time limit.
At the end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and
you return to where you joined from.

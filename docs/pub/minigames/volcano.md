# Volcano

Avoid the flame warnings, magma impacts and holes. Be the last survivor.

Flames warn you before each impact. Touching impact magma or falling below the floor eliminates you and makes you a spectator.

Use `/volcano list` to find an arena, `/volcano join <arena>` to play,
`/volcano spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/volcano leave` to return.

The HUD shows the countdown, your progress or score, and the round winner. At the
end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and you return
to where you joined from.

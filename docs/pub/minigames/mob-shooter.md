# Mob Shooter

Shoot chickens for 3 points, rabbits for 2 and pigs for 1. Score the most before time runs out.

Your arrows replenish during play. Targets are replaced after a hit, and the highest score at timeout wins.

Use `/mobshooter list` to find an arena, `/mobshooter join <arena>` to play,
`/mobshooter spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/mobshooter leave` to return.

The HUD shows the countdown, your progress or score, and the round winner. At the
end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and you return
to where you joined from.

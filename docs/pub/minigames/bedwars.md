# BedWars

## Your goal

Protect your team's bed, break the other teams' beds, and be the last team with players still in the game.

While your bed is safe, you can return after being defeated. After an enemy breaks it, your next defeat is final. Work together: one player can defend while another collects supplies and builds toward an enemy island.

## Quick tips

* Cover your bed before rushing away.
* Build railings or crouch near narrow bridge edges.
* Watch the game display for beds, teams, and remaining players.
* A winner [Gift](../survival/items-and-crafting/gifts.md) or supply drops may be enabled for an arena.

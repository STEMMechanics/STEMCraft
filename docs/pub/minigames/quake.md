# Minecraft Quake

Right-click your railgun hoe to tag opponents. Wait for the reload between shots and reach the kill target first.

Shots stop at solid cover. After being tagged, you respawn with a brief protection period; you cannot fire during protection.

Use `/quake list` to find an arena, `/quake join <arena>` to play,
`/quake spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/quake leave` to return.

The HUD shows the countdown, your progress or score, and the round winner. At the
end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and you return
to where you joined from.

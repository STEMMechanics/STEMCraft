# Punch the Bat

Punch bats with an empty hand. Glowing bats score 5 points; ordinary bats score 1.

Use an empty hand. Bats are replaced after a successful punch, and the highest score at timeout wins.

Use `/punchthebat list` to find an arena, `/punchthebat join <arena>` to play,
`/punchthebat spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/punchthebat leave` to return.

The HUD shows the countdown, your progress or score, and the round winner. At the
end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and you return
to where you joined from.

# Boat Race

## Your goal

Steer your boat through the checkpoints in order and cross the finish after completing every lap. The game tracks your progress and finishing place.

## Quick tips

- Turn gently before a corner instead of waiting until you reach it.
- Do not skip a checkpoint; the finish only counts after the course is complete.
- Look ahead at the track, but check the display when you need your lap or checkpoint progress.

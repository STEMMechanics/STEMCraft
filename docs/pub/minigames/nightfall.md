# Nightfall

## Your goal

Prepare during daylight, then survive waves of zombies at night. The nights grow harder, and players have a limited number of lives. Players who fall during a night can move around while dead and return at sunrise if they still have a life.

## Blood Moons

Blood Moons are much more dangerous. Later Blood Moon zombies can carry TNT and overcome simple water traps by moving nearby soft blocks such as dirt or mud. Their destructive abilities increase as the game goes on.

At later stages, a treasure-bearing [comet](../survival/comets.md) may cross the sky and crash inside the arena. **Take cover.** The impact is deadly, but its geode and scattered minerals can reward a brave team after the explosions finish.

## Quick tips

- Use the day to collect, build, repair, and regroup.
- Do not depend on one water channel or a wall made only from soft blocks.
- Save safe escape routes for Blood Moons.
- A match can choose a different gathering lobby each time, but everyone in that match starts together.

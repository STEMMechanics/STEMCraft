# Bridge

## Your goal

Join the red or blue team, build across the gap, and enter the other team's goal to score. Stop opponents from reaching your goal. The team that reaches the arena's winning score first wins.

## Quick tips

- Build a safe route your teammates can reuse.
- Defend your goal when opponents get close.
- Falling is not the end of the round—you can return and try another route.
- Watch the display for each team's score.

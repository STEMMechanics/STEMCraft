# TNT Run

## Your goal

Keep moving while the floor disappears behind the runners. If you fall through the arena's layers and are eliminated, you can watch the players who remain. The last runner standing wins.

## Quick tips

- Spread out so another player does not cut away your path.
- Make small turns and look for larger patches of floor.
- Save sprint jumps for gaps you cannot walk around.
- Watch the countdown—the round can also have a time limit.

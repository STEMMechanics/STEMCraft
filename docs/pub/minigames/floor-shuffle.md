# Floor Shuffle

Move onto the announced colour before the other tiles disappear. Keep surviving as the warnings get shorter.
Tiles that will disappear flash white twice first; the safe colour stays unchanged.

The floor returns and shuffles after each colour challenge. Leaving the arena bounds
eliminates you and makes you a spectator; you remain in play while falling inside
the arena. Each colour is announced in matching-colour chat and a two-second title.

Use `/floorshuffle list` to find an arena, `/floorshuffle join <arena>` to play,
`/floorshuffle spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/floorshuffle leave` to return.
Players wait in the lobby during the starting countdown and move onto the floor
when it ends, including when the arena allows a single player. By default, you
then have 15 seconds to get ready on the solid floor before the first colour
challenge. The HUD shows when play will start.
The floor cycles through random colour patterns while players wait in the lobby.

The HUD shows the countdown, your progress or score, and the round winner. At the
end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and you return
to where you joined from.

# Floor Shuffle

Move onto the announced colour before the other tiles disappear. Keep surviving as the warnings get shorter.

The floor returns and shuffles after each colour challenge. Falling below the floor eliminates you and makes you a spectator.

Use `/floorshuffle list` to find an arena, `/floorshuffle join <arena>` to play,
`/floorshuffle spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/floorshuffle leave` to return.

The HUD shows the countdown, your progress or score, and the round winner. At the
end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and you return
to where you joined from.

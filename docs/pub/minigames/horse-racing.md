# Horse Racing

Jump obstacles and ride through all checkpoints and laps before the other riders.

Every rider gets a horse with the same speed and jump strength. Dismounting or leaving the course returns you to your last checkpoint. Pass every gate in order; the last gate counts a lap.

Use `/horserace list` to find an arena, `/horserace join <arena>` to play,
`/horserace spectate <arena>` to watch, or `/horserace leave` to return.

The HUD shows the countdown, your progress or score, and the round winner. At the
end of the match, your previous inventory and game mode are restored and you return
to where you joined from.

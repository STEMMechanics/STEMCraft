# Minefield

## Your goal

Cross the field and reach the finish without stepping on a hidden mine. Safe and dangerous spaces are revealed as the round is played, so slow observation can beat a fast guess.

Finishing adds a completion bonus to your score. The exact field size, mine pattern, and blocks can change between arenas.

## Quick tips

- Watch where successful players step.
- Pause when the path ahead has not been tested.
- Check the game display for mines and your progress.

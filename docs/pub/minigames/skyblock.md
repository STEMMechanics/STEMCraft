# SkyBlock

## Your goal

Start on a tiny floating island and carefully turn its limited materials into a growing home. Your island is kept for you between visits unless it is deliberately reset.

## Quick tips

- Look inside the starter chest before changing the island.
- Crouch near edges so you do not walk off.
- Save saplings and renewable resources.
- Build a platform under risky work when you can.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Resetting an island is destructive. Do not choose a reset option unless you mean to start again.
{% endhint %}

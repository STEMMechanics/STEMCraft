# Coins

\
STEMCraft uses a coin currency for trading. Below are some of the coins you will find around the world for trading.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/STEMCraft-Coins.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Copper Coin** - The least valuable coin, worth around 1 Gravel block

**Iron Coin** - Worth around 2 Copper coins

**Gold Coin** - Worth around 4 Iron coins or 8 Copper coins

**Diamond Coin** - Worth around 4 Gold coins or 32 Copper coins

**Emerald Coin** - Worth around 4 Diamond coins or 128 Copper coins

There is no way to craft coins with each coin being tracked on the server. Players who use exploits to duplicate a coin will lose their entire inventory!

# Traders

STEMCraft Survival worlds contain not only vanilla villagers, but also special wandering villagers known as **traders**.

There are several types of traders in the STEMCraft world and they appear randomally along your travels.

### Item Trader

An item trader is defined with a chest above their head. They are happy to purchase items from you and sell you items they have available.

The items they have available is dependant on several factors such as where and when the trader bought it and how many they have. A trader may require to be sold a minimum about of an item before they make it available to other players.

### Pet Trader

_Pet traders are currently being considered_

Similar to Item Traders, these traders are marked with a paw above their head. They will sell you pets, some pets are just for decoration while others can be used in combat.

# The Hub

The Hub is the place to choose your next activity. Use `/hub` to return, and `/help` to summon [STEMBot](stembot.md).

| Destination | Hub coordinates | What to do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Survival and noticeboard | -871, 85, 346 | Explore Survival or read community notices |
| Creative | -864, 85, 236 | Build on your own plot |
| Minigames | -836, 85, 289 | Choose from the available arenas |
| Monthly challenge and voting | -902, 85, 289 | Read the current brief, dates and voting instructions |

You can also use `/survival` or `/creative`. Read the local signs: the monthly challenge and the arenas currently open can change.

## Community notices and voting

The [noticeboard](survival/notice-boards.md) helps players find projects, trades and requests. Keep notices friendly and never include personal information.

When challenge voting is open, right-click a choice's lever to vote; click it again to retract that vote. You cannot vote for your own entry. `/vote status` shows voting status and your allowance. Visit the entries before deciding: votes recognise the effort other builders put into their work.

# Getting Help

If this handbook does not answer your question:

* Ask in the [STEMMechanics Discord](https://discord.gg/yNzk4x7mpD).
* Email [hello@stemmechanics.com.au](mailto:hello@stemmechanics.com.au).
* Use `/report <message>` for behavior that staff should review.

News, events, galleries, and the staff team are maintained on STEMMechanics channels rather than in this handbook.

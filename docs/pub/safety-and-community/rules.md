# Rules & Expectations

1. Be kind, civil, and constructive.
2. Use an appropriate player name and avatar; do not impersonate others.
3. Do not spam chat, commands, or mentions.
4. Respect staff time and do not unnecessarily mention or message them.
5. Do not advertise or self-promote without permission.
6. Protect your own and other people's personal information.
7. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, sexual content, or illegal content.
8. Do not grief, steal, or destroy other players' work where the world's rules prohibit it.
9. Do not enter restricted areas or use cheats, hacks, or exploits to bypass barriers.
10. Use common sense and follow the spirit of these rules; loopholes do not make harmful behavior acceptable.

Consequences depend on context and severity and may include warnings, restrictions, removal from the server, temporary bans, or permanent bans. See current [Public Punishments](punishments.md).

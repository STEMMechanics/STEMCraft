# FAQs

## Where can I get help or answers?

If you cannot find what you are after in this wiki or have a question, don't hesitate to reach out at one of the following channels:

* Through the [STEMMechanics Discord server](https://discord.gg/yNzk4x7mpD)
* Sending us an email at to [hello@stemmechanics.com.au](mailto:hello@stemmechanics.com.au)
* Contact us in game, start your message with @mod to talk to any moderators that maybe online.

## Can I play on the STEMCraft server?

Why yes, you can get started playing on the STEMCraft server for free.

When you first join, you maybe asked to enter a code in chat before you can do anything.

## What do I need to play on the STEMCraft server?

* You require a full, licensed copy of Minecraft for PC or Mac (Java Edition)
* We do not support Tablet (Bedrock) version at this time due to the customizations the server runs
* The version of Minecraft is typically the latest version. Check the [News](news.md) page for updates

## Is there any cost to play?

No. The STEMCraft server is free to play. Some workshops held by STEMMechanics however may have a cost to participate

## Can you add \[plugin/idea/mob/feature] onto the server?

**Short answer:** maybe. Contact us using one of the various methods and we will see what we can do.

**Long answer:** STEMCraft is used by a lot of people and organisations for a variety of different reasons and any changes or features we add need to take this into account. We also need to ensure that the gameplay and mechanics work for players of any age and that the game is fair and balanced for everyone.

We do encourage people to send us feedback and suggestions on the direction you would like to see STEMCraft take, however at times it may not be as simple as installing a new plugin or resource pack, we may need to recreate the feature from scratch in code to work, and to work correctly within the creative and survival regions, battle arenas and quest instances.

## Are there world resets?

We try to avoid world resets, especially in the Survival world as players spend a lot of time gathering resources and building. We instead set hard world limits and increase them when a new version is released to spawn new biomes and features.

This means that new items can be a fair distance away, this is helped by the use of [Waystones](custom-items/waystones.md) and [City](survival-cities.md) portals.

In the future we may create new survival worlds that are generated fresh that players can teleport between. More information will be available when we decide to go down this road.

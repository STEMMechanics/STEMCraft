# Rules

**To participate on the STEMCraft server, please follow the rules below.**

1. :grinning: Be cool, kind, and civil. Treat all members with respect and express your thoughts in a constructive manner.
2. :card\_index: Use an appropriate name and avatar. Avoid special characters, emoji, obscenities, and impersonation.
3. :incoming\_envelope: Do not spam. Avoid excessive messages, images, formatting, emoji, commands, and mentions.
4. :no\_bell: Do not unnecessary mention or direct message staff. Respect their time, they are real people too.
5. :loudspeaker: No self-promotion or advertisements. This includes unsolicited references and links to other social media, servers, communities, and services in chat or direct messages.
6. :shield: No personal information. Protect your privacy and the privacy of others.
7. :head\_bandage: No harassment, abuse, or bullying. We have zero-tolerance for harming others.
8. :anger\_right: No racist, sexist, anti-LGBTQ+, or otherwise offensive content. We have zero-tolerance for hate speech.
9. :rotating\_light: No piracy, sexual, NSFW, or otherwise suspicious content. We do not condone illegal or suspicious discussions and activities.
10. :imp: No Griefing, stealing, or destroying other players' creations. Don't create impossible obstacles (breaking through to the void) and destroy the experience of other players (this **DOES NOT** apply in the Survival world)
11. :lock: Don't enter areas you are not meant to be in, such as a locked door or the Developers area. Using cheats or hacks to get around barriers is not on.
12. :thinking: Rules are subject to common sense. These rules are not comprehensive and the use of loopholes to violate the spirit of these rules is subject to enforcement.



**Depending on the severity of the breach, users can face the following actions:**

* An official warning
* Temporary account bans
* Account restrictions and limitations
* Permanent account bans

## Warnings

Players can receive warnings either automatically by the server or from [moderators](../faqs.md#moderators) for breaking the rules in a non-serious manner.

If you receive too many warnings from moderators, you will end up temporarily or permanently banned from the server.

| Warning # | Action                  |
| --------- | ----------------------- |
| 1 - 2     | Warning                 |
| 3         | Kicked from the Server  |
| 4         | 30 minute temporary ban |
| 5         | 5 hour temporary ban    |
| 6         | 7 day  temporary ban    |
| 7 - 9     | 1 month  temporary ban  |
| 10        | Permanent ban           |

{% hint style="info" %}
Warnings currently accumulate over the lifetime of the player and do not expire.
{% endhint %}

## Bans

### Temporary

If you break the rules in what is considered a serious manner, or receive a number of warnings within a short period of time, your account will be temporarily banned for 1 week.

Any permissions you hold, such as [moderator](../faqs.md#moderators) or [builder](../faqs.md#builder) may also be removed from your account.

### Permanent

Permanent bans can and will be issued for extremely serious breaches, destruction of server content, hacking or circumventing imposed restrictions.

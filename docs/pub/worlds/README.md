# Worlds and exploration

Use `/survival` for resource gathering and adventure, `/creative` for plot building, and `/hub` to choose another activity. Creative and Survival have separate inventories; Creative is not a way to supply Survival items.

Survival exploration portals lead to four custom-generated realms. Availability depends on the server. These are different from the Hub's destination portals.

| Realm | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Wasteland | Dry plains, mesas, craters, ruins and rare oases |
| Skylands | Floating islands at several heights, with real void between them |
| The Deep | Sealed-roof caverns, pillars, shelves, lakes and lower lava |
| Faraway Lands | Distorted walls, spires and shelves, with calmer terrain near the centre |

Read [Exploration portals](portals.md) before taking valuable equipment through. Features are generated in new terrain; already explored chunks do not change when the plugin is updated.

## Named regions and the map

STEMCraft gives discovered biome territories and structures names. The coordinate bar can show the area you are in. Where enabled, Pl3xMap displays **Biome Regions** and **Discovered Structures** layers; use its layer controls to show or hide them.

The Deep's custom map view shows the first exposed cavern surface beneath the roof, not every cave level. Old gray tiles may remain while a map update is being rendered. A map is a guide, not proof that a route is safe.

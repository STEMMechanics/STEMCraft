# Quests

Look for a quest NPC with a **yellow question mark**. Right-click the NPC and follow its offer to start. Your quest book contains the story, objectives and progress.

1. Read the quest book before travelling or collecting items.
2. Complete the objectives in order. A quest might ask you to collect items, visit a place, meet someone or defeat mobs.
3. Follow the yellow exclamation mark when an NPC is your next objective or your quest is ready to hand in.
4. Return to the correct NPC carrying your current quest book and any requested items. Right-click to hand in.

Some quests consume the requested items. Time-limited quests and repeatable quests have their own deadlines or cooldowns; check the book. Completing an objective does not necessarily mean you have handed in the quest.

## Tracking and stopping

The final book page has tracking and abandon actions. Java players can use the clickable buttons. On Bedrock, type the commands printed in the book: `/quest track <quest-id>` or `/quest abandon <quest-id>`. Use `/quest` for the available player choices and `/quest view <quest-id>` to view an active quest's book.

Quest books belong to the player who accepted them. Someone else's book cannot transfer their progress to you. Abandoning ends that attempt; an old book cannot be used to claim its reward later.

The [coordinate bar](sleeping-and-navigation.md) is useful while exploring. If you get lost, ask `/help` about quests rather than abandoning immediately.

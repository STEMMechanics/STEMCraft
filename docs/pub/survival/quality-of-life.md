# Quality of Life

The current plugin includes these configurable Survival improvements:

* Sneak-right-click a mature crop with a hoe to harvest and replant a 3×3 area of that crop.
* Players and mobs do not trample farmland.
* An identical stack from your main inventory refills a consumed or placed held stack; item metadata must match.
* Powered rails accelerate minecarts beyond vanilla behavior, up to a configured limit.
* Leads do not break merely because of distance.
* Disconnected leaves decay faster.
* Right-click a named mob to see its type, health, name, and owner information.
* Tools and armour warn as they cross configured low-durability thresholds.
* Anvils warn before Minecraft's 40-level “Too Expensive” limit.
* The End Dragon can respawn automatically after a configured delay in eligible End worlds.

These features are configurable, so the live server may enable them only in selected worlds.

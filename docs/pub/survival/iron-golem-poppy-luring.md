# Lead Iron Golems with Poppies

Need to move an Iron Golem? Hold a normal **Poppy in your main hand**. Golems within about **10 blocks** become interested and walk after you.

- The Poppy is not consumed.
- Natural and player-built Iron Golems can follow.
- Several golems can follow one player.
- When several players hold Poppies, a golem prefers the closest one without constantly changing its mind.
- Put the Poppy away or walk too far away to stop leading it.
- Fighting and protecting villagers always matter more. A golem immediately stops following a Poppy when it has an enemy to attack.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A Poppy does not make an angry Iron Golem friendly. Never approach one that wants to attack you.
{% endhint %}

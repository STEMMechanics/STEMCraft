# Farming, Foraging & Cooking

![Rice farming and tropical cooking on an island](../images/rice-farming-and-cooking.png)

The Cooking collection adds 64 ingredients, prepared components, meals and drinks. Accept the server resource pack so each item has the correct appearance. All crafting-table recipes below are shapeless: the ingredients can go in any slots.

## Farming and foraging

Only naturally generated plants provide foraged ingredients. Leaves, grass and ferns placed by players do not produce extra drops. Fortune improves the marked leaf, seaweed and rice harvests.

| Item | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Rice Shoots | Use shears on seagrass in swamps, mangrove swamps, warm oceans or lukewarm oceans. |
| Rice | Plant Rice Shoots on Mud with source water directly above, at light level 9 or higher. Mature rice drops 2–4 Rice and 1–2 replacement shoots; Fortune helps. |
| Edible Seaweed | Use shears on seagrass or kelp in oceans, warm oceans or lukewarm oceans; Fortune helps. |
| Coconut | Break jungle leaves on beaches or in jungles and sparse jungles; Fortune helps. |
| Banana | Break jungle leaves in jungles, bamboo jungles or sparse jungles; Fortune helps. |
| Mango or Lime | Break jungle leaves in jungles or sparse jungles; Fortune helps. |
| Pineapple | Break short grass or ferns in jungles, bamboo jungles or on beaches. |
| Ginger | Break short grass or ferns in jungles, swamps or mangrove swamps. |
| Chilli Pepper | Break short grass or ferns in savannas, savanna plateaus or jungles. Eating one raw hurts half a heart. |
| Tomato | Break short grass or ferns in plains, sunflower plains, forests or taigas. |
| Onion, Garlic or Fresh Herbs | Break naturally generated short grass or ferns. |

Rice grows while its chunk is loaded. Break a mature plant to harvest it.

## Potion of Haste

Add an **Amethyst Shard** to Awkward Potions to brew **Potions of Haste I** lasting 3 minutes. Add Redstone Dust for **Long Potions of Haste I** lasting 8 minutes, or Glowstone Dust for **Strong Potions of Haste II** lasting 1 minute 30 seconds.

The live server may adjust drop chances, growing times, recipes and effect durations. Its active data-pack configuration is authoritative.

# Recipes

STEMCraft supports shaped, shapeless, furnace, blasting, smoking, campfire, stonecutter, smithing-transform and brewing recipes. Players can also discover active recipes through Minecraft's recipe book.

The handbook lists the recipes supplied with the server by collection:

* [Farming, Foraging & Cooking](farming-and-cooking.md)
* [Useful Rotten Flesh](rotten-flesh-uses.md)
* [Fried Eggs](fried-eggs.md)
* [Slime and Magma Buckets](slime-and-magma-buckets.md)
* [Mailboxes](../mailboxes.md)

The live server's data-pack configuration is authoritative and may differ temporarily while a collection is being updated.

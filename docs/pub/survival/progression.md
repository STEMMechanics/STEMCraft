# Skills, unlocks and badges

Use `/skills` (or `/professions`) to see your profession levels, and `/skills <skill>` for progress toward the next level. `/stats` shows a broader activity report.

Mining, Herbalism, Farming, Fishing, Cooking, Engineering, Melee Combat and Ranged Combat each progress separately. Skills start at level 1 and currently go up to level 100. Early levels are quick; later levels require more lifetime XP. Creative and Spectator activity does not earn profession XP.

## Convenience unlocks

These are the bundled defaults. The live server may retain different requirements after an update.

| Unlock | Required profession level |
| --- | --- |
| Replace a broken tool from matching inventory stock | 5 in an eligible tool/combat profession |
| Named-animal information, where enabled | Farming 5 |
| Stronger leads | Farming 8 |
| Refill a used-up held stack | Engineering 10 |
| Harvest and replant a 3×3 crop area with a hoe | Farming 15 |
| Faster powered minecarts | Engineering 15 |
| Automatic tool selection | Mining **and** Engineering 20 |
| Vein mining | Mining 25, with a diamond or netherite pickaxe |
| Tree felling | Herbalism 25, with a diamond or netherite axe |

Durability warnings, anvil warnings and crop-trampling protection do not require these unlocks. See [Quality of Life](quality-of-life.md) for how to use the features.

## Badges

Badges recognise milestones such as play time, quests, building and minigame achievements. Some awards, including community event wins, are granted by staff. They are not evidence of administrator status. Your badge icons can appear beside your name; the resource pack makes them visible.

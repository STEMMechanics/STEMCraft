# Animal Barrels

Hold an ordinary barrel and sneak-right-click a chicken, rabbit, frog, or cat to capture it. The filled barrel is unstackable and cannot be placed as storage. Right-click a block with it to release the animal into the adjacent space and recover the ordinary barrel.

The barrel preserves the animal's name, age, tame owner, and supported variant. Only the owner can capture a tamed animal unless special permission has been granted.

{% hint style="info" %}
This works with chickens, rabbits, frogs, and cats—not every animal. A filled animal barrel is for carrying the animal, not storing items.
